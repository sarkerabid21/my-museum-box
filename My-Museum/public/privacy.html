My Museum

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

My Museum (“we”, “our”, or “the App”) provides a private 3D virtual museum where signed-in curators can organize artworks, customize avatars, and invite selected guests to explore their gallery.

1. Information We Collect

  • Google Account Profile: When you sign in with Google, we receive your basic profile details (display name, email address, and profile photo) via Firebase Authentication solely to identify your private museum and verify invited access.
  • Museum Content: Artworks, room names, gallery metadata, avatar preferences, and invite permissions you create are stored securely in Google Cloud Firestore and restricted to you and the guests you explicitly invite.
  • Live Presence: When you walk inside a shared museum, your avatar position and display name are temporarily shared with other authorized visitors in that same museum.

2. Optional Gmail Permission

Basic sign-in does not request access to your Gmail account. Only when a museum owner explicitly chooses to email an invitation pass to a guest does the App request the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.send permission to send that single invitation email on your behalf after your confirmation. We never read, store, or scan your inbox, and the temporary access token is kept strictly in memory for the current session.

3. How We Use & Protect Data

Your data is used exclusively to run your virtual museum experience. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third-party advertisers. All museum records are protected by Firebase Authentication and Firestore security rules.

4. Your Choices & Deletion

You may delete uploaded artworks, revoke guest access, or sign out at any time within the App. You may also revoke Google OAuth permissions at any time from your Google Account Permissions page.

5. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or want to request account data deletion, please contact the developer at abidsarker213@gmail.com.
